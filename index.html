Operational notes // read once, remember forever

SURVIVE THE SIGNAL.

The facility is generated as you move. Every run changes the sequence, but the entities follow rules. Learn the tells; don't just react to the noise.

01 / MOVEMENT

AD or arrow keys to switch lanes. Your runner commits to a short sidestep, so plan early instead of panic-spamming.

02 / VAULT

SPACE jumps gaps and broken floor plates. The timing matters; jump as the edge reaches you, not when it first appears.

03 / SLIDE

SHIFT or C ducks under low beams. Sliding also lets you skim through some narrow hazards.

04 / THE PEELER

Red lane markings and a rising stinger mean a charge is coming. Get out of the marked lane before it hits.

05 / THE LISTENER

When it announces “STAY STILL,” stop changing lanes, jumping, and sliding until the signal passes. The corridor still carries you forward.

06 / THE REARWARD

When “BEHIND YOU” flashes, hold R while it passes. Looking away is what it wants you to do.

07 / PROGRESSION

Every fifth yard offers a choice of a run-changing upgrade. Shards and recovered logs persist between runs. Thirty yards ends the Standard run; Marathon continues until you fall.

08 / SURVIVAL

You have three hearts by default. Damage briefly protects you from another hit, but the invulnerability window is short. A clean run is worth more than a fast one.
